Frequently asked questions

Is it a subscription? Will I be charged every month?+

No. Atomic Method is not a subscription: you pay once and it's yours forever. There are no monthly fees or automatic charges, and you never pay for Atomic Method again. The only monthly cost is your Claude or ChatGPT account, which is separate and up to you.

Does it work on my phone?+

You can buy it on your phone, but to make the videos you need a Mac or Windows computer, because Atomic Method runs in the Claude or ChatGPT desktop app. Then you send the finished video to your phone and upload it like always.

Do I have to pay for an AI separately?+

Yes: Atomic Method works inside Claude Pro or ChatGPT Plus, which cost about $20 a month and are paid directly to them. If you already pay for one of them, you don't spend anything extra. Atomic Method is paid just once.

What exactly do I get?+

A .zip folder with the Atomic Method system, a step-by-step guide to install it and all the packs. Open the folder in your AI, type “configúrame” (Spanish for “set me up”) and it gets everything ready.

What do I need to use it?+

A Mac or Windows computer, a paid Claude account (Pro or higher) or ChatGPT account (Plus or higher) with its desktop app, and an ElevenLabs key for the voice, which has a free plan to get started. You can also use a free voice that runs on your computer.

Do I need to know how to code?+

No. You type to your AI like a normal chat. The guide takes you step by step.

Do I have to be on camera?+

No. Everything is drawn and animated. You don't film anything.

Is there a video or credit limit?+

There are no credits or top-ups in Atomic Method. You use your own AI account, so you can make as many videos as your AI account allows.

What languages does it make videos in?+

In the language you choose when you set it up: English, Spanish (Latin American or from Spain) and others. The voice and captions come out in that language.

Can I make videos for my clients?+

Yes. The videos you make are yours, including for client work.

How do I get Atomic Method after paying?+

As soon as your payment is confirmed, you get an email with your access. Download the folder, open the “Start here” guide and follow the steps: in a few minutes your AI is ready to make your first video. If you get stuck, message us and we'll help.
