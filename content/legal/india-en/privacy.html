Payments are processed by Whop. We use your name and email only to give you access, send you updates and provide support. Atomic Method runs on your computer: your videos, your files and your keys never go through us.

What data we use

  • Purchase data: the name, email and country you enter when you pay. Your card details go directly to Whop; we never see or store them.
  • Browsing data: this page uses the Meta pixel (Facebook and Instagram) and the TikTok pixel, with their cookies, to measure which ads work. We also send those events to Meta and TikTok from our server. They may include your IP, your browser, your approximate location and, if you buy, your email and name encrypted (they can't be read).

Who it's shared with

Only with the services needed to sell to you and give you access: Whop (payments), Vercel (page hosting), Meta and TikTok (ad measurement). We don't sell your data.

Your options

You can block cookies in your browser and turn off personalized ads in your Meta and TikTok settings. To ask us to delete your data, message our Instagram.
